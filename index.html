Sachindra Gamage, Ph.D.

Applying analytical chemistry to questions about life beyond Earth.

I am an analytical chemist expanding my research into astrobiology, bringing experience in molecular analysis, separation science, instrumentation, and life-science applications to questions related to biosignature detection and the search for life beyond Earth.

My research interests lie at the intersection of analytical chemistry and astrobiology, where sensitive analytical approaches can help characterize molecular signatures in complex terrestrial and planetary-analog environments.
