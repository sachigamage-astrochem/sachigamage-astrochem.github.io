01 · About

From molecular analysis to astrobiology.

My scientific background spans analytical chemistry, life-science applications, instrumentation, and molecular analysis. Much of my previous work has focused on developing analytical technologies for detecting and characterizing molecular targets in complex biological samples.

I am now expanding that foundation into astrobiology, where I am interested in how sensitive analytical methods can be applied to the detection and characterization of potential biosignatures in complex terrestrial and planetary-analog environments.

This direction brings together my experience in separation science, mass spectrometry, analytical instrumentation, molecular detection, and life-science research with questions surrounding life detection beyond Earth.

02 · Research Direction

Where chemistry meets the search for life.

Biosignature detection

Developing and applying analytical approaches to detect molecular signatures that may provide evidence of biological activity.

Separation science & mass spectrometry

Using high-sensitivity chemical analysis to resolve and characterize complex mixtures relevant to life-detection studies.

Planetary analog environments

Studying challenging terrestrial environments as test beds for understanding how potential biosignatures may be preserved and detected.

Molecular data & bioinformatics

Integrating chemical and biological information to better interpret signatures of life in complex environmental samples.
